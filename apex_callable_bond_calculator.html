How the calculator works (and what it leaves out)

Issuer NPV test: At the call date, the present value of the remaining original coupons and principal is calculated using the refinancing market rate. Apex compares that value with the call price. NPV benefit = PV of keeping the old bond − call price. A positive result means replacing the old bond at the call price has a lower present-value cost, assuming replacement borrowing is available at the stated market rate.

Investor view: Annual reinvestment income is estimated by investing the call proceeds at the new market rate. The market value estimate is the present value of the original bond's remaining promised payments at that rate. If that value exceeds the call price, the difference illustrates the investor's potential opportunity cost from being called. It is not a complete realized-return calculation.
